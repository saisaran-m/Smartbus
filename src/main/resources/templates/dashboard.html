Get started with the SmartBus app

On the SmartBus app, there are 3 easy ways to find, track, and ride buses across Tamil Nadu.

1. Find your bus with "From" & "To" search bar

  1. On your Android device or mobile browser, open the SmartBus app 🚌.
  2. In the "From" search bar, enter or tap the city you want to start your trip from (e.g. Chennai, Salem, Coimbatore).
  3. In the "To" search bar, enter your destination stop (e.g. Tiruvannamalai, Madurai, Trichy).
  4. Tap "🔍 Find Buses":
    • Check all available TNSTC & SETC buses running between your selected stops.
    • View bus type (Ordinary, Express, Deluxe, AC Sleeper).
    • See exact departure time, arrival time, and live crowd level (Low, Medium, High).

2. Live Map Tracking & Smart Stop Guardian

  1. On any search result card, tap "📍 Track Bus".
  2. The live Google Maps radar opens showing the bus's genuine route and moving GPS coordinates.
  3. Stop Guardian: Tap "🔔 Enable Stop Guardian".
    • SmartBus calculates your distance to destination.
    • Sends high-priority vibrations and audio alerts 10 min, 5 min, and 1 min before your stop so you never oversleep.

3. Booking Digital Tickets & Instant UPI

  1. Tap "🎫 Book Seat" on any bus.
  2. Pick your preferred window or aisle seat in the visual bus layout.
  3. Select your UPI App (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM) to pay directly to saisaran0070@oksbi.
  4. Enter your 12-digit UPI UTR reference to verify and issue your confirmed Digital Boarding Pass with scannable QR code.
  5. Your ticket stays saved offline in "My Tickets" even without internet.

4. Camera Incident Reports & Lost Baggage Recovery

  • 📸 Live Camera Reports: Open the 3-line hamburger menu, choose "Report Bus Issue", and snap a real photo of speeding or dirty conditions to report directly to the transport cell.
  • 🎒 Lost Baggage: Left a bag on the bus? Open "Lost & Found Baggage" from home to alert the terminal station master and conductor before the bus departs again!
